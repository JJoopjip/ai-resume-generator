Project Manager, Growth Programs
Location: Toronto, ON (Hybrid)

About the role
We are hiring a Project Manager to lead cross-functional growth programs from
concept to launch. You will coordinate marketing, e-commerce, product, and
operations partners, manage scope, schedule, and budget, and turn performance
data into decisions. This is a delivery-focused role for someone who ships.

What you'll do
- Plan and deliver cross-functional programs across Agile and Waterfall teams
- Manage scope, schedule, budget, and risk across multiple workstreams
- Coordinate marketing, e-commerce, product, and operations stakeholders
- Track KPIs and translate performance into clear recommendations
- Support new channel launches and partner integrations

What we're looking for
- 4+ years of project or program management, ideally in consumer or fintech
- Track record of on-time, cross-functional delivery
- Comfort with KPIs, business reviews, and stakeholder communication
- PMP or equivalent an asset
- Master's degree an asset
